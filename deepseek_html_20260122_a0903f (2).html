From the First Day

Rahisha 🌸
❤️

I still remember the very first day we spoke. Not because something dramatic happened, but because something quietly changed inside me — something I didn’t even understand at that moment.

What started as small conversations, simple debates, and moments that probably felt ordinary to you, slowly became important to me.

The way you spoke with confidence, the way you expressed yourself, the way you stayed true to who you are — all of it stayed with me.

I didn’t plan to feel this way. It wasn’t sudden, and it wasn’t forced. My feelings grew slowly, naturally, without me even realising when it happened.

There were many times I wanted to say something… and many times I chose silence instead. Not because I didn’t care, but because I cared enough to be careful.

I never wanted to rush you. I never wanted to make you uncomfortable. I never wanted to become someone who pressured you. So I kept choosing respect — again and again — even when my heart wanted more.

This page isn’t meant to impress you. It isn’t meant to trap you in a moment. It’s simply the most honest way I know how to express what I’ve been carrying quietly.

I like you, Rahisha. Not just for a moment, not just for a reason — but from the first day… till now.

Whatever you choose — yes or no — I promise one thing will never change: the respect I have for you, and the sincerity behind these words.
